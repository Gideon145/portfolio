About

Gideon Opukeme, Cybersecurity Engineer, Aberdeen, Scotland

I am Gideon Opukeme, a cybersecurity engineer based in Aberdeen, Scotland, currently completing an MSc in Cyber Security at the University of Aberdeen. I build detection and response tooling: an LLM prompt injection firewall, a genetic algorithm fuzzer that discovers jailbreak bypasses, an autonomous SOC analyst, and Sigma detection rules written for Splunk, Sentinel, QRadar and Elastic environments.

Before cybersecurity I was among the earliest developers on OKX.AI, deployed six autonomous security agents, and won the platform's inaugural global hackathon with Parry Protocol. I've contributed detection rules to SigmaHQ and Elastic, shipped smart contracts with a perfect ChainGPT audit, and hold Google and Fortinet certifications.
